# KDE artwork

These files are unchanged copies of KDE artwork. The models in this repository are derivatives of them.

- `kdeapp.svg`: the KDE logo, from the [Breeze icon theme](https://invent.kde.org/frameworks/breeze-icons) (`icons/apps/48/kdeapp.svg`, the target of `kde.svg`). License: [LGPL-3.0-or-later](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/lgpl-3.0.html), © 2014 Uri Herrera and others (see `COPYING-ICONS` in breeze-icons).
- `start-here-kde.svg`: the Plasma logo, from the Breeze icon theme (`icons/places/64/start-here-kde.svg`, the target of `start-here-kde-plasma.svg`). License: [LGPL-3.0-or-later](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/lgpl-3.0.html), © 2014 Uri Herrera and others (see `COPYING-ICONS` in breeze-icons).
- `Konqi.svg`: Konqi, the KDE mascot, designed by Tyson Tan, converted to SVG by Franco Perez. Source: https://community.kde.org/File:Konqi.svg. License: [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) (the KDE Community Wiki's license for its content; Tyson Tan's Konqi artwork is released under CC BY-SA, GFDL and LGPL).
