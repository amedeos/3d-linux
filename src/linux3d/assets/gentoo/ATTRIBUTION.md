# Gentoo artwork

These files are unchanged copies of the official Gentoo artwork. The models in this repository are derivatives of them.

- `Gentoo-logo-peach-plain.svg`: the Gentoo "g" logo, vector version (plain) by Matteo Pescarin, after the logo by Lennart Andre Rolland and the Gentoo Foundation. License: [CC BY-SA 2.5](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.5/). Source: https://wiki.gentoo.org/wiki/File:Gentoo-logo-peach-plain.svg
- `Larry-the-cow-full.svg`: Larry the Cow, male edition, by Ethan Dunham and Matteo Pescarin. License: [CC BY-SA 2.5](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.5/). Source: https://wiki.gentoo.org/wiki/File:Larry-the-cow-full.svg
- `Znurt.svg`: Znurt, the Gentoo flying saucer ("Znurt v1.0.1"), by the Gentoo Foundation, Inc. The original artwork is by Daniel Robbins; the vector remake is by Sebastian Pipping (2011). License: [CC BY-SA 2.5](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.5/). Source: https://wiki.gentoo.org/wiki/File:Znurt.svg

"Gentoo" and the Gentoo "g" logo are trademarks of the Gentoo Foundation, Inc. and of Förderverein Gentoo e.V. Their use follows the [Gentoo Name and Logo Usage Guidelines](https://www.gentoo.org/inside-gentoo/foundation/name-logo-guidelines.html). The models scale the logo uniformly and never distort it.
