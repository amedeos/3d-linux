# Tux artwork

These files are unchanged copies. The models in this repository are derivatives of them.

- `penguin-flat.svg`: Tux, the Linux penguin, in three colours (black, white, yellow), by Larry Ewing (the original drawing, made with The GIMP) and Simon Budig (the vector version, made for silk screen printing). Source: http://www.home.unix-ag.org/simon/penguin/
- `README`: Simon Budig's notice that comes with the drawings. It must be shared together with them.
